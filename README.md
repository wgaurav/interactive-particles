# Palm Promax Gold — Animation

**[▶ View Live Demo](https://wgaurav.github.io/interactive-particles/)**

Interactive 3D gold bar built with Three.js and custom GLSL shaders.

---

**Move your cursor** over the bar to interact — lighting follows your position and brightens the nearest particles.

**Scroll** to watch the bar animate through its full sequence.

On mobile, tap and drag to interact.
